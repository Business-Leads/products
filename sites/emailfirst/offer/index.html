Frequently Asked Questions

Is the trial the full service?

Yes. It is the complete email campaign described on our main site, run for one month. LinkedIn management is not part of the trial but can be added if you continue.

What happens at the end of the month?

The campaign stops. If you would like to continue, it carries on at £499 a month with no minimum term. There is no automatic renewal.

Who is it for?

UK businesses that sell to other businesses. Campaigns target decision makers at UK limited companies.

How soon does it start?

Your offer is developed in week one and sending begins in week two. Your first morning reports follow within days of the first send.

What do I need to do?

One call to develop your offer and agree who you want to reach, then approve the campaign before it goes out. After that, your time goes on following up the people in your morning report.

Can I take the trial more than once?

No. The £100 month is available once per business.
