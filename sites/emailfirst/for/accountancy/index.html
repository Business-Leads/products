Advisory work rarely arrives by referral

Most new clients reach your practice by recommendation, and most of them want what the last one wanted. Year-end accounts, a corporation tax return, perhaps payroll. It is steady work, but compliance fees are hard to raise, and partner time goes on deadlines rather than advice.

The work you would rather grow looks different. Monthly management accounts, cash flow forecasts, a part-time finance director for a company that has outgrown its bookkeeper. The owners who need it run established owner-managed companies, and many of them still think of their accountant as the person who files the accounts. So they never ask.

Approaching those owners yourself can feel awkward for a practice that guards its reputation. EmailFirst makes the introduction for you, briefly and professionally, and tells you each morning who was interested.

How it works for a practice like yours

  1. 1

    We shape your advisory offer with you

    We start with a consultation on the services you want to grow, the clients you want more of and why owners choose your practice. You approve the offer, the audience and every email before anything is sent.

  2. 2

    Brief, professional emails to owners

    Your campaign goes to owners and managing directors of UK limited companies that fit the clients you want. Each email is short and identifies your practice. They are sent from our platform, so your own domain and mailbox are untouched, and replies are forwarded to you.

  3. 3

    Named owners every weekday morning

    A morning report lists the owners who opened or clicked your emails, with their job title, company and contact details, so you can decide who to approach and how.

  4. 4

    A LinkedIn profile that shows an adviser

    Your profile is rewritten in week one, and from week two three posts a week go out in your voice. When an owner from your report looks you up, your profile and recent posts speak to the advisory work they read about. Nothing is published without your approval.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this sit comfortably with our professional reputation?

Every email is short, identifies your practice and includes an opt-out. Campaigns go only to corporate subscribers at limited companies, in line with UK regulations. You approve the offer, the audience and every email before anything is sent.

Will the emails come from our own domain?

No. They are sent from our platform, separate from your own domain and mailbox, and replies are forwarded to you.

Who will the emails go to?

Owners and managing directors of UK limited companies that match the clients you want, agreed with you at the start. We supply the data, so you need no list of your own.

How much partner time does it take?

An initial consultation to develop your offer and agree your audience. After that, around ten minutes a week to approve your LinkedIn posts, and whatever time you choose to give to the owners in your report.

Can you approach the owners on LinkedIn for us?

Yes. LinkedIn is part of the service. Engaged owners from your report are approached from your own profile, and qualified conversations are passed to you the same working day.

How many advisory clients should we expect?

Results depend on your offer and market, so we do not quote a fixed number. The sums are yours to judge. The email campaign is £499 a month, so one new client paying £10,000 a year in advisory fees would more than cover a year of it.
