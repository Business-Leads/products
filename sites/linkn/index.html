Questions people ask

How much of my time does it take?

A 45-minute call to start, then about ten minutes a week approving your posts. After that, your time goes on the conversations.

How soon does it start?

Your profile is rewritten in week one and posts start in week two. Conversations begin in weeks three to four, once there is something recent on your profile for people to see.

How many meetings should I expect?

On the Business plan and above, plan on a handful of real conversations a month once things are running, and more on Growth, where your best contacts are also followed up by telephone. LinkedIn limits how many people one profile can approach each week, so it is a steady build rather than a flood. I would rather set that expectation now than promise a number I cannot control. Your monthly report shows exactly what happened.

Will it sound like me?

Yes. Posts are written from your own posts, notes and way of speaking, and nothing is published until you have approved it.

How do you look after my account?

I work at a sensible pace, well within what LinkedIn expects of an active professional, and build up gradually. Your account, your connections and your conversations stay yours throughout.

Why not just do this myself?

You could. It is about fifteen minutes a day, every day, plus the writing. The difficulty is that it is the first thing to go the moment a client needs something, and LinkedIn rewards showing up week after week rather than in bursts. If you already post three times a week and follow up every connection, you do not need me.

Whose LinkedIn account is used?

Yours. Everything goes out from your own profile, so the connections and the relationships stay with you.
